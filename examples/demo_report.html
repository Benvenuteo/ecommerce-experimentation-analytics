02 / Randomized experiment (Hillstrom)

The Hillstrom randomization supports intention-to-treat causal contrasts between each campaign and the no-email group, under the experiment's original design. Primary endpoint: 2-week conversion. Two-sided p-values for two comparisons are Holm adjusted. Unadjusted 95% Newcombe intervals show uncertainty in each risk difference; spend-per-assigned-customer intervals use nonparametric bootstrap and are exploratory. No costs are supplied, so profit/ROI are not identified.

03 / Interpretation and guardrails

Business decision rule: prior to rolling out a campaign, examine adjusted significance, the effect size, the interval of plausible values, incremental revenue and the unknown delivery and merchandise costs. Never interpret a positive point estimate alone as sufficient proof of commercial gain.

Separation of datasets: REES46 customer identities and Hillstrom customer identities are unrelated and are never joined. Observational event analytics cannot establish the causal effect of emails. Experimental estimates cannot be transferred automatically to another marketplace or era.

Historical context: Hillstrom is a 2008 experiment; REES46 covers 2019–2020. Neither dataset represents current G2A customers.
